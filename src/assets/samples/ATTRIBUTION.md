# Sample photos

The app's three sample disease cases use real photos when these files are in this folder:

| File name | Shows | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `fall-armyworm.jpg` | Maize leaves/whorl damaged by fall armyworm (caterpillar or ragged feeding holes) | Fall Armyworm sample |
| `maize-streak.jpg` | Maize leaf with the pale yellow streaks of Maize Streak Virus | Maize Streak Virus sample |
| `tobacco-frog-eye.jpg` | Tobacco leaf with round "frog-eye" Cercospora leaf spots | Frog-Eye Leaf Spot sample |

`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` and `.webp` all work. If a file is missing, the app falls back to a drawn illustration. The fourth sample (the unclear photo) stays an illustration on purpose.

Keep each photo under about 500 KB (resize to roughly 1000 px wide) so the app stays fast on mobile data.

## Only use photos you have the right to use

This repository is public. Use one of:

- **Your own photos**, ideally taken in Malawian fields. This is the best choice.
- **Photos from an extension officer or your university**, with their written permission.
- **Openly licensed photos** (CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA), for example from
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org). Search the disease name and check the
  license in the file's "Licensing" box. Avoid "NC" (non-commercial) and "ND" licenses.

Do not copy photos from extension websites, Google Images, or Plantwise unless they say you may reuse them.
